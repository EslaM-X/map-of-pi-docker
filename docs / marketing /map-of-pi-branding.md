# Brand-Safe AI Assets Checklist
**Map of Pi — Draft v1.0**

## Purpose
Ensure all AI-generated visual assets remain creative **without compromising trust, clarity, or brand recognition**.

## 1️⃣ Logo Usage & Safe Area
- The Map of Pi logo must always remain **fully visible and unobstructed**.
- Maintain a **minimum clear space** around the logo equal to **50% of the logo height** on all sides.
- Do **not** crop, stretch, rotate, or distort the logo.
- AI variations may alter style (illustrative, flat, 3D), but **core logo shape and proportions must remain intact**.

## 2️⃣ Minimum Size Requirements
- **Digital assets:**  
  - Minimum logo width: **64px**
- **Video / Social thumbnails:**  
  - Minimum logo width: **96px**
- Logo must remain legible on mobile screens at first glance.

## 3️⃣ Text & Layout Rules
- Logo must **never overlap text, charts, or UI elements**.
- Avoid placing the logo near busy backgrounds that reduce contrast.
- Text must not cross or interfere with:
  - Logo
  - Trustometer gauge
  - Trust indicators

## 4️⃣ Color Constraints
- Use approved brand colors whenever possible.
- AI-generated color variations are allowed **only if**:
  - Contrast remains high
  - Logo is still instantly recognizable
- Avoid color combinations that:
  - Reduce readability
  - Conflict with trust or warning semantics (e.g., red overload)

## 5️⃣ Trustometer Guidelines
- Trustometer is a **core trust signal**, similar to a gauge or pedal.
- Visual variations are allowed, but:
  - Orientation and meaning must stay clear
  - Scale and labeling should remain consistent
- Do not over-stylize in a way that confuses its purpose.

## 6️⃣ Export & Performance Standards
- **Image size target:** 70–100 KB  
- **Formats:**  
  - PNG (transparent assets)  
  - WebP (recommended for web performance)  
- Avoid megabyte-sized exports unless absolutely required.
- Optimize assets to support fast load times and SEO.

## 7️⃣ AI Content Review Checklist (Before Publishing)
- [ ] Logo shape preserved  
- [ ] Safe area respected  
- [ ] No text overlap  
- [ ] Colors within brand constraints  
- [ ] Trustometer meaning clear  
- [ ] File size optimized  
- [ ] Mobile-friendly preview checked  

## 8️⃣ Approval Flow
All marketing assets must be shared in the **marketing channel** and approved by:
- Antony  
- Flick  
- Danny  

Before external publishing.

## Notes
This checklist is intended to **enable creativity**, not limit it — ensuring Map of Pi assets remain trusted, recognizable, and scalable as AI tools evolve.
